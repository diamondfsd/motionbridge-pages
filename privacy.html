PRIVACY

Privacy policy

Effective date: September 7, 2026

Summary

MotionBridge is designed to route motion locally between a supported input and a supported camera or gimbal. MotionBridge does not require an account, use advertising trackers, or sell personal information.

Information processed

  • Motion data: phone rotation or supported headphone motion is used to calculate and route control signals. It is processed for the active session and is not sent to a MotionBridge-operated cloud service.
  • Connection data: Bluetooth, local-network, Wi-Fi, and device status data are used to discover and communicate with devices that you choose to connect.
  • Speech input: if you enable the voice assistant, microphone audio is used for live speech recognition. MotionBridge does not save the audio. The operating-system speech service or an AI endpoint that you explicitly configure may process the input under its own privacy policy.
  • Local preferences: settings such as language, selected devices, and user-enabled connection preferences may be stored on the device. Credentials are stored only when you explicitly choose the app's remember option.

Sharing and retention

MotionBridge does not sell or share personal information for advertising. Session motion and connection data are kept locally as needed for the feature and are removed when you clear the app data or uninstall the app. If you configure a third-party AI or speech service, that provider's collection, retention, and use are governed by its terms and privacy policy.

Permissions

Bluetooth, local network, nearby-device, motion, microphone, and speech-recognition permissions are requested only for features that need them. You can change these permissions in system settings; disabling them may prevent the related feature from working.

Children and changes

MotionBridge is not directed to children under 13. We may update this policy when the app or applicable requirements change. The effective date above will be updated with material changes.

隐私

隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 9 月 7 日

摘要

MotionBridge 用于在支持的输入设备与相机或云台之间本地传递动作数据。MotionBridge 不要求注册账号，不使用广告追踪器，也不会出售个人信息。

处理的信息

  • 动作数据：使用手机旋转数据或支持的耳机动作数据计算并传递控制信号。在当前会话中处理，不会发送到 MotionBridge 运营的云服务。
  • 连接数据：使用蓝牙、本地网络、Wi-Fi 和设备状态数据，发现并连接你主动选择的设备。
  • 语音输入：启用语音助手后，麦克风音频用于实时语音识别。MotionBridge 不保存音频。操作系统语音服务或你主动配置的 AI 服务可能根据其自身隐私政策处理相关输入。
  • 本地偏好：语言、已选设备和用户启用的连接偏好等设置可能保存在设备上。只有在你明确选择记住时，连接凭据才会保存。

共享与保留

MotionBridge 不会为广告目的出售或共享个人信息。会话动作数据和连接数据按功能需要保存在本地；清除应用数据或卸载应用后会移除这些数据。如果你配置第三方 AI 或语音服务，相关数据的收集、保留和使用由该服务的条款与隐私政策约束。

权限

蓝牙、本地网络、附近设备、运动、麦克风和语音识别权限，仅在对应功能需要时请求。你可以在系统设置中修改权限；关闭权限可能导致相关功能不可用。

儿童与政策变更

MotionBridge 不面向 13 岁以下儿童。应用功能或适用要求变化时，我们可能更新本政策；发生重大变化时会更新上方生效日期。
